About

How we make our assets

Built for real projects

Every pack is tested in Unity before release: prefabs with sensible pivots and real-world scale, LODs and colliders set up, and validated with Unity's Asset Store tools.

Original designs

Our buildings are inspired by architectural styles, not copied from real buildings. No model is a replica of a real, named building, and textures are checked by hand for text, logos and signs.

Open about our tools

The 3D models are built procedurally in Blender from hand-written building specs. Concept art and facade textures were made with the help of AI image generation (FLUX.1 [dev]) and reviewed by hand.
